A pharmaceutical quality system under FDA CGMP guidance is built on three interconnected core elements:

• Management Responsibility – Senior management defines quality objectives, allocates resources, establishes organizational roles, ensures personnel qualifications, and conducts regular reviews to oversee quality outcomes.

• Production System Controls – This element involves process validation, in-process controls, environmental monitoring, equipment qualification, and deviation handling procedures that trigger corrective and preventive actions when needed.

• Laboratory and Documentation Controls – This includes validating analytical methods for specificity, accuracy, and reproducibility; maintaining laboratory data integrity; and ensuring comprehensive documentation (batch records, SOPs, and change control) to provide a traceability chain from raw materials to finished product.
