According to FDA CGMP guidance, a pharmaceutical quality system is a robust and interconnected system that integrates current good manufacturing practice (CGMP) requirements with a systematic approach to process understanding and risk management. Several core elements comprise this system. First, **management responsibility** requires senior management to establish quality policy objectives and allocate resources, including defining roles, ensuring personnel qualifications, and conducting periodic management reviews of quality outcomes. Second, **production system controls** focus on demonstrating consistent manufacturing results through process validation, utilizing in-process controls, environmental monitoring, and equipment qualification. Deviation handling procedures are crucial for addressing unexpected events and instigating corrective and preventive actions based on root cause analysis. Finally, **laboratory and documentation controls** ensure data integrity through validated analytical methods, complete and attributable records, and traceability through batch records, standard operating procedures, and change control documentation.
